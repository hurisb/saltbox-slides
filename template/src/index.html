How it works

Five steps

From a rough idea or an old deck to a live link, in one conversation.

  1. 01

    Tell Claude what you need

    Notes, an old deck, a PowerPoint, or just a topic

  2. 02

    Answer a few questions

    Audience, goal, how it will be presented, and the facts to check

  3. 03

    Review the first three slides

    Approve the look before the whole deck is built

  4. 04

    Get the full deck

    One HTML file, with speaker notes and thumbnails

  5. 05

    Share it

    Open it locally, or deploy it to Vercel for a link

  • The skill always stops after three slides so you can approve the direction cheaply.

Layout · Signal cards

What Claude will ask you

AudienceWho is in the room

  • Prospects, clients, AEs, or leadership
  • Presented live or read alone

GoalOne sentence

  • What should people do after the last slide?

SourceWhat you have

  • Notes, a doc, a Google Slides link, or a .pptx
  • Keep the words, or tighten them

Facts to confirmBefore anything ships

  • Numbers that disagree between sources
  • Years, names, and client logos you can show

LengthTime and depth

  • How many minutes you have
  • Which parts matter most

SharingWhere it goes

  • A local file, a Claude link, or Vercel
  • Public or internal content
  • The full question list lives in references/questions.md.

Layout · Panel and columns

Convert a PowerPoint

Example request

"Convert sales-kickoff.pptx with saltbox-slides"

Claude extracts every slide's text, images, and speaker notes, shows you what it found, then rebuilds the deck on the Saltbox theme.

What you get

  • Your content, tightened and restructured if you want
  • Speaker notes carried over
  • Images reused where they still fit
  • Brand backgrounds, type, and icons

What Claude does

  • ExtractRuns scripts/extract-pptx.py to read slides, notes, and images
  • ConfirmShows the outline and flags conflicting facts
  • BuildThree slides first, then the rest after you approve
  • DeliverOne HTML file, plus a Vercel link if you want one
  • Google Slides works too: share a link, or download it as .pptx and attach it.

Keyboard shortcuts

Move between slides

→ or ↑ next · ← or ↓ back · Space next · Home and End jump · 1–9 go to a slide

Thumbnails

⌘B on Mac, Ctrl+B on Windows. Click any thumbnail to jump to it.

Present

F for fullscreen, which hides the thumbnails. N shows speaker notes.
